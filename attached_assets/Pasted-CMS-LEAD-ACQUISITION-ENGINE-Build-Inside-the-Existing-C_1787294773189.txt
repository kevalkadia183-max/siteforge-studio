2. PRODUCT PURPOSE

The new feature is an AI Website Sales Engine.

Its purpose is:

Find real businesses that do not have a proper website, analyze their online presence, qualify them as website prospects, collect permitted business information and images, generate a personalized website preview using our existing CMS, and prepare/send personalized outreach through supported email and WhatsApp Business integrations.

Example:

A user searches:

Carpenter — Waterloo, Canada

The system finds:

Heidel Carpentry

It discovers that the business has:

* business name
* category
* location
* phone
* reviews
* rating
* services
* photos
* social/business information
* no dedicated website

The system then:

1. Creates a lead.
2. Calculates an opportunity score.
3. Collects verified/permitted public business information.
4. Uses the existing CMS to generate a prospect website.
5. Creates a preview URL.
6. Generates personalized email outreach.
7. Generates personalized WhatsApp outreach.
8. Allows the user to review/edit before sending.
9. Sends through compliant supported integrations.
10. Tracks outreach and responses.
11. Allows conversion of the prospect into a real CMS customer.

9. WEBSITE DETECTION

This is one of the most important components.

Do NOT simply check whether a URL exists.

Classify the business into:

NO WEBSITE

No dedicated business website detected.

BROKEN WEBSITE

Website exists but is unavailable/broken.

POOR WEBSITE

Website exists but has serious issues.

MOBILE PROBLEMS

Website has significant mobile usability problems.

OUTDATED

Website appears old/outdated.

GOOD WEBSITE

Website appears to provide a reasonable current business presence.

UNKNOWN

Unable to determine reliably.

Never tell the user that a business has “no website” unless the system has reasonable evidence.
